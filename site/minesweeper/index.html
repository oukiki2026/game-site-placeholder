Minesweeper.
Read the neighbours.

A small browser game with a safe opening, touch-friendly flag mode and no account or prizes.

Click or tap to reveal. Right-click to toggle a flag, or turn on Flag mode before tapping. Flags are your guesses, not confirmed mine locations. Start a new game to reset.

How to win

Reveal every safe cell without opening a mine. A number counts mines among the surrounding eight positions (fewer at an edge). Opening an empty region reveals its connected empty cells and boundary numbers. The first revealed cell and its neighbours are safe; mines are placed after that first reveal.

Two useful deductions

If a 1 touches only one unopened cell, that cell is a mine. If the number of correctly flagged neighbouring mines equals a revealed number, its remaining neighbours are safe. The second rule depends on your flags being correct; a misplaced flag can make a later move unsafe.

When there is no forced move

This version does not guarantee a guess-free board. Sometimes more than one arrangement fits the visible numbers. No solver or auto-click system is provided. A flag marks a possibility; it does not validate your reasoning.

Privacy and controls

This game runs in memory in the current tab. Reloading starts over. No score, account, wager or leaderboard is collected. Keyboard users can Tab to a cell and press Enter or Space; Flag mode applies to keyboard activation too.

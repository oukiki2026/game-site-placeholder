DAILY SUDOKU · NO ACCOUNT NEEDED

A fresh puzzle.
A little clearer thinking.

A unique-solution Sudoku for each day, with local progress and an archive from our launch date.

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with 1–9 without repetition. Given numbers cannot be edited. Use the keyboard or tap a cell. A reveal supplies an answer, not a logical explanation.

Daily puzzles and archive

A new dated puzzle is generated locally each day at 00:00 UTC. No server job or account is needed. Dates before launch on 2026-10-03 are unavailable. Use the date selector to replay a previous puzzle; the same date always produces the same puzzle. Progress is saved only in this browser where local storage is available.

Start with a single

Choose an empty cell and rule out digits already present in its row, column and box. If one digit remains, that is a naked single. If a digit fits only one empty cell in a unit, it is a hidden single. Both deductions follow directly from the game rules.

Difficulty and fair play

Each puzzle is checked for exactly one solution by the generator. We do not label puzzles easy or hard based only on blank counts. Some may require methods beyond the beginner notes. There is no leaderboard, prize, paid entry or claim of cognitive benefit.
